  `organise`, `travelling` and `personalised`.
- Keep task titles as actions, for example `Review`, `Confirm`, `Choose` and `Set up`.
- Keep official organisation names next to links.
- Do not add claims such as `easy to use`, `validated` or `students preferred` unless
  supported by real target-user evidence.
- Use `release candidate`, not `final validated release`.
